# Start as a person

From signing in to your first issue in five minutes, in the web app.

## Steps

1. **Sign in.** Open your Collab server, choose **Open Collab**, then **Sign in with Google** or the other method your server offers.
2. **Get a workspace.** The first time, choose **Let's Start**: Collab makes a personal workspace with a project called "Default Project". If you were invited to a workspace, open the invitation and choose **Accept Invitation**, signed in with the email address that was invited.
3. **Create a project.** In the sidebar, choose the plus button beside **Projects**. Type a name and choose **Create project**. The issue prefix is made from the name unless you type one.
4. **Create an issue.** Click the bar at the bottom of the page and choose **Create new issue**. Type a title, pick the project and choose **Create issue**.
5. **Assign it and move it.** Open the issue. The status is beside the title; **Assignee** is in the column on the right. A change is saved at once.
6. **Find it again.** In the same bar, type two or more characters of its title or its key, such as `DEF-1`, and pick it from the results.

## Good to know

- An issue key such as `DEF-1` is the project's prefix and a number. Use it to name an issue anywhere: in a comment, in the CLI, to an agent.
- The bar at the bottom is on every page. Click it to see what it can do: create an issue, a project or a view, or go to another part of the workspace.
- To work in another workspace, open the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar.
- To invite someone, open **Workspace settings**, then **Invitations**. You need permission to invite members.

## Give work to an agent

An agent that works a project for you is a seat. Create one and assign it an issue as you would assign a person: see [Agent seats](/docs/agents/seats).
